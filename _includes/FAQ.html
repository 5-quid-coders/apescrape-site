Frequently asked questions

The questions you may or may not have.

How do I tell ApeScrape what to look for?
You build a query: give each field a name and describe it in plain English. Our AI extracts exactly those fields from every page.
Do I have to list every page I want to search?
Not at all. Just add a website and ApeScrape reads through the pages for you.
What if there are duplicates?
ApeScrape detects and merges duplicate records into a single clean row for you, automagically.
Can I keep my data up to date?
Yes. Give a collection a schedule and ApeScrape re-runs it for you automatically, so your data is never stale.
Can I work with my team?
Absolutely. Create a workspace, invite your teammates and share your collections and results across everyone.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You describe what you want in plain English, and there's nothing to install or script. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can use ApeScrape.
What kind of data can I collect?
Anything you can describe: names, prices, dates, deadlines, contact details, descriptions and more, pulled together from across many pages and sites.
Is the information collected publicly available?
ApeScrape is built for gathering information that is already public on the open web. You're responsible for how you use it and for respecting each site's terms.
Where do my results go?
Everything ApeScrape collects appears in clean, structured tables inside your workspace that you can sort and explore.
